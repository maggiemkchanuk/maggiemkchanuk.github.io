Children’s Book Awards

A reading guide for anyone looking for their next children’s book, with a focus on picturebooks and inclusive literature. Start with the 2026 results, then follow the award organisers’ links to explore shortlists and earlier winners.

United Kingdom

Carnegie Medal for Writing

An annual medal for outstanding writing in a book for children or young people. The medal has been awarded since 1936.

2026: Wolf Siren by Beth O’ Brien

2026 winners

Carnegie Medal for Illustration

The annual illustration medal was previously known as the Kate Greenaway Medal. Its archive brings the winners under both names together.

2026: Wiggling Words by Kate Rolfe

Illustration winners archive

Klaus Flugge Prize

Launched in 2016, this prize celebrates a newcomer to children’s picturebook illustration for their first published picturebook as an illustrator.

2026: Seven Babies by Forest Xiao

2026 winner and prize information

Inclusive Books for Children Awards

These awards celebrate inclusive books published in the UK for children aged one to nine. The shortlists are a good starting point for exploring representation across age groups.

2026 winners by category

International

Hans Christian Andersen Award

IBBY presents this award every two years to a living writer and a living illustrator. It honours their body of work and its lasting contribution to children’s literature.

2026: Michael Rosen for writing and Cai Gao for illustration

IBBY’s 2026 announcement

United States

Randolph Caldecott Medal

The Association for Library Service to Children (ALSC) honours the artist of a distinguished American picturebook for children each year.

2026: Fireworks, illustrated by Cátia Chien and written by Matthew Burgess

2026 winners and honour books

John Newbery Medal

Awarded annually by ALSC for a distinguished contribution to American literature for children. The medal is named for the eighteenth-century bookseller John Newbery.

2026: All the Blues in the Sky by Renée Watson

2026 winners and honour books

Schneider Family Book Awards

The American Library Association recognises books that portray disability experiences through writing or illustration. It gives awards for younger children, middle-grade readers and young adults.

2026 winners and honour books

Australia

CBCA Book of the Year Awards

The Children’s Book Council of Australia recognises Australian children’s literature across several categories. For picturebooks, explore Picture Book of the Year, Early Childhood and New Illustrator.

2026: Once I Was a Giant by Zeno Sworder (Picture Book of the Year)
